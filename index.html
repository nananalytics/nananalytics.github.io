AI disclosure framing and user verification

Business Experimentation & Causal Methods, Jan–Mar 2026

+43.5 ppRisk-oriented messaging consistently raised verification, with the largest effect among engaged users.

  • A/B testing
  • Causal inference
  • Regression
  • Qualtrics

Repository coming soon

Question
When people lean on AI-generated content under time pressure, does the way the AI is disclosed change whether they stop to check it?
Data
A randomized experiment I designed in Qualtrics with 73 participants, built around a realistic task with a deadline.
Method
Defined verification click rate at the verify-or-submit decision as the outcome. Ran randomization checks with a proportion z-test, tested covariate balance, and fit regressions with controls.
Why it matters
Small wording changes in a product can reduce over-reliance on AI outputs without blocking the user.

U.S. childcare affordability by county

Machine learning project, Jan–Mar 2026

Distinct county profilesSegments defined by childcare burden, income, labor participation, and price volatility.

  • Python
  • pandas
  • scikit-learn
  • PCA
  • K-Means

Repository coming soon

Question
How does the cost of childcare differ across U.S. counties once you account for local income and labor markets?
Data
34,000+ county-level childcare price and socioeconomic records, with about 30% of values missing.
Method
Engineered affordability and price-premium features, handled the missing values, then reduced dimensions with PCA and clustered counties with K-Means.
Why it matters
The segments show where families face the heaviest burden, which is where support and new supply would help most.
